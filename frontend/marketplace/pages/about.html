ArtAround

What is ArtAround?

Esplora l'arte in modo nuovo

ArtAround is a web application made for exploring museums all over the world. Firstly, either log in or register to access to the Marketplace where you can search for the museum you intend to visit. Click on the museum and choose to create a new personalized tour or follow an existing one. If you choose to create a new visit you will be directed to an editor page where you are free to create your own custom experience. Otherwise you will be rerouted to the Navigator app which will allow you to start the guided tour. Navigate through the museum using vocal commands and ask the app to give you insights on the artworks you are admiring or the author who realized them.

Il Progetto

ArtAround is the Unibo's Web Technologies project for the academic year 2025/26 created by Serra Federico, Mazzettti Matteo and Ziccone Andrea
