About ArtAround

ArtAround is a generic software suite for cultural heritage that brings museums and galleries closer to their visitors. Curators, teachers and independent authors publish guided visits in the Marketplace; visitors explore them on their phone with the Navigator companion app.

Each visit adapts to who is taking it — interests, background, time available and age — so the same artwork can be told a hundred different ways. No two visits are alike.
